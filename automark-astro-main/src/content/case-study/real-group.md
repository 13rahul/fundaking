    **Real Group India** spans multiple businesses — including **Real Chakki Fresh Atta** and related group companies. Visitors landing cold needed to understand **who the group is**, **which business they need**, and **how to enquire** — not an org-chart PDF maze.

challenges:
  title: "Challenges"
  content: |
    **Many brands, one story.** Subsidiaries had their own histories; the group site had to unify them without flattening what makes each business distinct.

    **Visitor-first navigation.** Legacy structure followed internal departments. Buyers and partners think in products and locations, not reporting lines.

    **Trust and contact.** FMCG and corporate buyers expect proof, clarity, and a direct way to reach the right team.
