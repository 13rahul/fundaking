overview:
  image: "/images/case-studies/shubhshrey-logo.png"
  title: "Overview"
  content: |
    **Shubhshrey Industries Private Limited** needed more than a brochure site. Sales depended on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and manual research — slow, incomplete, and impossible to scale.

    Fundaking built the **backend and CRM** that runs their commercial pipeline, then layered **AI agents and APIs** so the team can find leads that match a defined buyer persona, enrich each contact with usable details, and run outreach from one system.

    The public brand site ([shubhshrey.com](https://www.shubhshrey.com/)) still captures inbound interest. The real step-change is **outbound automation**: find → enrich → qualify → outreach → CRM follow-up, without rebuilding the stack every quarter.
challenges:
  title: "Challenges"
  content: |
    **No shared CRM.** Leads lived in chats and inboxes. Nobody had a single view of open opportunities, stages, or history.

    **Manual lead research.** Finding buyers meant googling companies, guessing emails, and pasting LinkedIn links into sheets — incomplete records and wasted hours.

    **Weak persona fit.** Without a system that scored leads against a buyer persona, outreach volume went up while quality stayed flat.

    **No automation layer.** There were no APIs connecting enrichment providers, outreach tools, and an internal database — every step was copy-paste.

solution:
  image: "/images/case-studies/shubhshrey-crm.png"
  title: "Solution"
  content: |
    We designed one stack: a **custom CRM backend**, **AI agents** that hunt and score leads against buyer personas, **enrichment APIs** that fill contact fields, and **outreach automation** wired back into the same records.
  items:
    - "**Custom CRM backend:** Lead records, stages, notes, ownership, and order context — the system of record for sales."
    - "**AI lead agents:** Agents search and shortlist companies/people that match defined buyer personas (industry, role, geography, intent signals)."
    - "**Lead enrichment:** APIs and pipelines attach name, LinkedIn URL, email, company name, and company address to each qualified lead."
    - "**Outreach automation:** Sequences and send/reply hooks so follow-ups start from enriched CRM records, not blank sheets."
    - "**Integration APIs:** Internal APIs connect forms, agents, enrichment, and outreach so the whole loop can run without manual glue."
    - "**Public site bridge:** Enquiries from [shubhshrey.com](https://www.shubhshrey.com/) land as CRM records alongside agent-sourced leads."
