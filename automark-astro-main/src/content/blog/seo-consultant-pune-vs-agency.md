## The core difference in one sentence

An **SEO agency in Pune** sells coordinated execution at scale. An **SEO consultant in Pune** sells senior diagnosis, prioritization, and governance — usually with your team or partners doing the hours.

Neither is “better.” They fit different org shapes.

## When a Pune SEO consultant is the right call

Choose a consultant when:

1. **You have engineering or content capacity** but no SEO leadership — you need tickets, not another task list without owners.
2. **Your site is technically messy** — migrations, JS frameworks, index bloat, international hreflang — and you need someone who can argue with devs in their language.
3. **You want founder-level access** — the person who signs the strategy is the person on the call.
4. **You are pre-PMF or post-PMF SaaS** — you cannot afford six months of generic blogging; you need a focused cluster around product intent.

That is most of my Fundaking clients: Pune and India-based founders with small marketing teams and high standards for technical quality.

## When a Pune SEO agency is the right call

Choose an agency when:

1. **You need 20+ pieces of content per month** plus design, social, and paid media under one roof.
2. **You have no in-house dev** and need the vendor to implement via their bench or partners.
3. **You want a single invoice** for SEO, PPC, and creative — integration matters more than individual seniority.
4. **You are an enterprise** with procurement requirements, SLAs, and multi-location reporting dashboards.

Agencies win on throughput. The failure mode is **strategy dilution** — brilliant sales call, junior delivery. Mitigate that with contractual access to senior strategists and clear KPIs tied to revenue.

## The hybrid model many Pune teams use

The pattern I see work best:

- **Consultant** sets quarterly roadmap, technical priorities, and content architecture
- **In-house marketer** or **specialized agency** executes production and link outreach
- **Consultant** reviews releases, Search Console, and pipeline impact monthly

You pay for brains where it compounds and hours where it scales.

## Red flags specific to the Pune market

Whether consultant or agency, walk away if you hear:

- Guaranteed #1 for “SEO consultant Pune” (or any head term) in 30 days
- Pricing tied only to keyword count
- No questions about your product, sales cycle, or dev stack
- Cannot explain a recent **technical** win in plain language

## Mistakes I see on live sites

- Chasing tactics from Twitter threads without a baseline audit in Search Console and analytics.
- Publishing more blogs when the root issue is indexation, faceted URLs, or slow LCP on money pages.
- Treating AI visibility as separate from technical SEO — crawlers and models both need clean HTML and consistent facts.
- Skipping internal links from new posts to BOFU pages, so traffic lands nowhere useful.

## A 30-day workflow you can run in-house

**Week 1 — Baseline:** Export top landing pages by impressions, list five prompts or queries buyers actually use, screenshot current AI/search results for your brand.

**Week 2 — Fix facts:** Align pricing, integrations, and founder bio across site, GBP, and LinkedIn. One wrong number in an old blog comment can poison summaries.

**Week 3 — Ship one asset:** Pick a single page tied to “SEO Consultant Pune vs SEO Agency: Which Fits Your Stage?” and rewrite the lede, add a comparison table or checklist, improve internal links.

**Week 4 — Measure:** Track qualified leads or demo requests from organic — not rank positions alone. Re-run your prompt library and log citation changes.
