Indian SaaS founders do not need a 40-page SEO strategy deck. They need a six-month roadmap that protects demos while the product ships. This is the sequence I use for B2B SaaS from Pune / Baramati — remote across India.

## Month 0: freeze vanity, pick money URLs

List pricing, comparison, integration, and alternative pages. Confirm each returns 200, is indexable, and appears in the sitemap. If not, stop blogging.

## Months 1–2: technical + entity foundation

- Crawl money templates (especially JS-rendered marketing sites)  
- Align product names, pricing bands, founder bio across site and LinkedIn  
- Fix redirect chains and accidental noindex  
- Stand up the [KPI dashboard](/blog/seo-kpi-dashboard-for-founders)

## Months 3–4: BOFU depth, not blog volume

Rewrite or build:

1. One category definition hub  
2. Two comparison / alternatives pages using sales language ([prompt research](/blog/prompt-research-from-sales-calls))  
3. One implementation / integration guide  
4. Internal links from every new asset to pricing/demo  

Optional: AI Overview readiness on the five battleground queries.

## Months 5–6: cluster expansion + measurement

Expand only after BOFU pages convert. Add TOFU spokes that feed the hub. Review demos from organic monthly; cut anything that does not assist pipeline.

Most Series A teams succeed with consultant advisory + one marketer + eng tickets — see [in-house vs consultant vs agency](/blog/in-house-seo-vs-consultant-vs-agency). Full [SEO consulting](/seo-consulting-services) when prioritisation thrash is the bottleneck. Proof pattern: [SaaS demo requests](/case-study/saas-demo-requests).

## FAQ

**Can we compress this into 90 days?**  
Yes if engineering bandwidth exists and you skip TOFU volume.

**What if product marketing owns the site?**  
SEO still needs ticket access; otherwise the roadmap is theatre.

**Do we need local SEO as a SaaS?**  
Only for hiring/brand city pages — not as the growth engine.

**How does AI search change the six months?**  
Same foundation; add citation logging in months 3–6.

**When do we hire in-house SEO?**  
After the roadmap is clear and ticket volume is steady.

## Product-led content rules for SaaS

Docs and changelogs can rank if they are crawlable and linked from marketing. Do not duplicate docs as thin blogs. Integration pages should name the partner plainly and include setup steps in HTML.

## Competitive alternatives pages without legal pain

Be factual, dated, and fair. Use sales-approved comparison tables. Link to pricing. Avoid fabricated “we always win” claims.

## Hiring and geo pages

If you hire in Pune/Bengaluru, those pages are HR SEO — useful, but not the growth roadmap core. Keep them out of the six-month demo-critical path unless brand search is the goal.

## Leadership decisions around “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”

### Solo marketer path

You have ten hours a week. For “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”, pick one revenue URL this topic supports, write acceptance criteria for engineering (status code, visible copy, schema match), and ship a single scannable asset sales can forward after a call. Refuse new blog ideas until that URL is green in Search Console and linked from your cluster hub.

Track work in a Sheet with columns: URL, owner, due date, status, KPI (demos, calls, WhatsApp, or qualified pipeline). Review it in the pipeline meeting — not a separate SEO theatre call.

### Already-have-an-agency path

Use this article as a scorecard. Ask for evidence that the checks here are done on your money URLs. If the agency only returns keyword greenery, escalate once with a written 14-day recovery plan. If they cannot speak to the substance of “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”, change the operating model.

### Scaling-content path

Volume is not the strategy. Gate every new outline on: which prompt or query it supports, which URL owns it, and which demo stage it influences. “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)” should appear in the quarterly roadmap with a named owner and a kill criterion if pipeline does not move.

## Commercial QA gates for “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”

**Discovery**
- [ ] Five buyer prompts or queries documented from sales or support
- [ ] Primary URL chosen; competing URLs listed for consolidation
- [ ] Screenshot baseline of SERP / Maps / AI answer dated

**Execution**
- [ ] Technical gates on the primary URL pass (200, indexable, facts in HTML)
- [ ] First screen rewritten in buyer language, not internal jargon
- [ ] At least one table, process list, or FAQ block that can be quoted
- [ ] Hub ↔ spoke ↔ service ↔ proof links with descriptive anchors

**Proof and QA**
- [ ] Claims with numbers are dated or scoped to India / segment
- [ ] Organization and author facts match About and schema
- [ ] Sales reviewed the page and would send it to a prospect

**Measurement**
- [ ] KPI tile exists on the founder dashboard
- [ ] Biweekly or weekly review assigned
- [ ] Decision logged after 30 days: double down, fix, or cut

## Pushbacks I hear on “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”

**“We need more content first.”**  
Not if money URLs fail crawl or contradict pricing. Volume without architecture dilutes topical authority and wastes writers.

**“Our agency said rankings are up.”**  
Ask for demos and the five-query sheet. Rankings without pipeline are a vanity report.

**“AI will handle this automatically.”**  
Models amplify whatever facts you publish. Wrong NAP, wrong pricing, and JS-only answers get amplified too.

**“We are too early for SEO.”**  
You are not too early for clean money URLs and entity facts. You may be too early for a large content team — that is different.

## Calendar — first 45 days on “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”

## FAQ — implementation details for “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)”

**Does this replace the cluster hub article?**  
No — this spoke implements one job; the hub frames the pillar.

**How do we keep pages from going stale?**  
Revisit when pricing, packaging, or service area changes. Stale BOFU claims are worse than thin TOFU posts.

**What is the minimum evidence for case-study claims?**  
Named process and scoped outcomes you can defend on a sales call — not invented percentages.

**Should every blog have the same CTA?**  
No. Match intent: Maps/WhatsApp for local, demo for SaaS, audit call for consulting.

**When is “SEO Roadmap for SaaS Founders in India (6 Months)” not P0?**  
When crawl/index on revenue templates is broken, or when ops cannot handle leads. Fix those constraints first.
