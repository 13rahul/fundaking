Internal links are the cheapest ranking and conversion lever most teams underuse. This is the architecture I install for topical clusters on Fundaking-style sites and client SaaS/service properties.

## Hub-and-spoke rules

1. One **hub** per commercial topic (definition + links to all spokes)  
2. Each **spoke** links up to the hub with a descriptive anchor  
3. Spokes link sideways to 1–2 siblings — not a random footer dump  
4. Every educational URL links once to a **service** and once to **proof** when claims get specific  
5. Hubs list spokes; orphans are bugs  

Fundaking’s four pillars follow this map — see [content cluster strategy](/blog/content-cluster-strategy-b2b-india).

## Anchor text that helps humans and models

Use destination meaning: “technical SEO audit checklist”, not “click here”. Vary lightly; do not exact-match spam. In-body links beat only-nav links for discovery.

## Audit process (half day)

Crawl internal outlinks from hubs and money URLs. Flag: orphan spokes, hubs with <3 children, educational pages with zero BOFU link, over-optimised repeated anchors. Fix in batches of 20 links — measurable and reversible.

## Linking for AI citations

Assistants follow clear entity paths. A cited guide that never links to pricing/demo wastes the click. Conversely, stuffing five CTAs dilutes trust — one primary CTA.

## FAQ

**How many internal links per article?**  
Enough to cover hub + sibling + service/proof; typically 5–12 in-body links for long guides.

**Should we automate links?**  
Carefully. Bad automation creates spammy sidebars.

**Do footer links count?**  
Weakly. Prioritise contextual body links.

**What about orphan case studies?**  
Link them from services and related spokes — proof should not hide.

**Where to start on Fundaking’s model?**  
Open a pillar hub, ensure every spoke is listed and reciprocates.

## Navigation vs contextual links

Primary nav should stay small. Contextual links carry topical signals. Related-posts modules help only if they are relevant — random popular posts modules dilute.

## Link equity during redesigns

When IA changes, ship a redirect + internal link update together. Updating nav without fixing in-body links leaves soft 404 paths in old articles.

## Measuring link work

Track: orphan count, average clicks-to-money-URL from hubs, and ranking movement on spokes you recently linked. Anecdotes are not enough after 30 days.

## Engineering tickets that finish “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”

### Solo marketer path

You have ten hours a week. For “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”, pick one revenue URL this topic supports, write acceptance criteria for engineering (status code, visible copy, schema match), and ship a single scannable asset sales can forward after a call. Refuse new blog ideas until that URL is green in Search Console and linked from your cluster hub.

Track work in a Sheet with columns: URL, owner, due date, status, KPI (demos, calls, WhatsApp, or qualified pipeline). Review it in the pipeline meeting — not a separate SEO theatre call.

### Already-have-an-agency path

Use this article as a scorecard. Ask for evidence that the checks here are done on your money URLs. If the agency only returns keyword greenery, escalate once with a written 14-day recovery plan. If they cannot speak to the substance of “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”, change the operating model.

### Scaling-content path

Volume is not the strategy. Gate every new outline on: which prompt or query it supports, which URL owns it, and which demo stage it influences. “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO” should appear in the quarterly roadmap with a named owner and a kill criterion if pipeline does not move.

## Commercial QA gates for “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”

**Discovery**
- [ ] Five buyer prompts or queries documented from sales or support
- [ ] Primary URL chosen; competing URLs listed for consolidation
- [ ] Screenshot baseline of SERP / Maps / AI answer dated

**Execution**
- [ ] Technical gates on the primary URL pass (200, indexable, facts in HTML)
- [ ] First screen rewritten in buyer language, not internal jargon
- [ ] At least one table, process list, or FAQ block that can be quoted
- [ ] Hub ↔ spoke ↔ service ↔ proof links with descriptive anchors

**Proof and QA**
- [ ] Claims with numbers are dated or scoped to India / segment
- [ ] Organization and author facts match About and schema
- [ ] Sales reviewed the page and would send it to a prospect

**Measurement**
- [ ] KPI tile exists on the founder dashboard
- [ ] Biweekly or weekly review assigned
- [ ] Decision logged after 30 days: double down, fix, or cut

## Pushbacks I hear on “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”

**“We need more content first.”**  
Not if money URLs fail crawl or contradict pricing. Volume without architecture dilutes topical authority and wastes writers.

**“Our agency said rankings are up.”**  
Ask for demos and the five-query sheet. Rankings without pipeline are a vanity report.

**“AI will handle this automatically.”**  
Models amplify whatever facts you publish. Wrong NAP, wrong pricing, and JS-only answers get amplified too.

**“We are too early for SEO.”**  
You are not too early for clean money URLs and entity facts. You may be too early for a large content team — that is different.

## FAQ — implementation details for “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO”

**Does this replace the cluster hub article?**  
No — this spoke implements one job; the hub frames the pillar.

**How do we keep pages from going stale?**  
Revisit when pricing, packaging, or service area changes. Stale BOFU claims are worse than thin TOFU posts.

**What is the minimum evidence for case-study claims?**  
Named process and scoped outcomes you can defend on a sales call — not invented percentages.

**Should every blog have the same CTA?**  
No. Match intent: Maps/WhatsApp for local, demo for SaaS, audit call for consulting.

**When is “Internal Linking Architecture for SEO” not P0?**  
When crawl/index on revenue templates is broken, or when ops cannot handle leads. Fix those constraints first.
