# FundedFirm Trading Rules Explained: 5 Rules Every Funded Trader Must Know

Prop trading gives skilled traders access to firm capital without risking personal savings. At **FundedFirm**, rules are published on our **Trading Rules** page and on each **challenge plan** on the homepage—there are no hidden restrictions beyond what we list there.

Most funded account losses come from **drawdown breaches** or **prohibited trading behavior**, not from strategy alone. This guide explains the **five rule areas that matter most at FundedFirm** (not generic industry myths).

> **Important:** Limits vary by account type (**1 Step**, **2 Step**, **Instant**, **Instant Daily**). Always confirm your plan on the pricing widget before trading.

---

## 1. Maximum drawdown limits (daily and overall)

Drawdown is the most critical rule on any FundedFirm account. If your equity breaches the **daily** or **overall** limit—**including floating losses**—the account can be **terminated immediately**.

### FundedFirm limits by program (check your plan)

| Program | Profit target | Max daily loss | Max overall loss | Min profitable days |
|--------|----------------|----------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **1 Step** | 10% | 3% | 6% (fixed from starting balance) | 3 days |
| **2 Step** | Phase 1: 8% / Phase 2: 5% | 5% | 10% | 3 days |
| **Instant** | None | 3% **trailing** (equity high of day) | 5% **trailing** (from highest equity) | 5 days |

**How daily drawdown works (Step programs):** Daily loss is based on the **higher of starting balance or starting equity** at the beginning of the day. The limit is **fixed for that day** and does not rise if you make intraday profit.

**How overall drawdown works (1 Step example):** Overall loss is **6% of your initial balance** and stays fixed even if you profit. Your balance or equity must remain above **94%** of the starting amount at all times.

**Other framework rules (evaluations):**

- **Minimum trading days:** At least **3 separate trading days** per evaluation step (where applicable).
- **Inactivity:** **30 consecutive days** with no trades may result in the account being disabled.
- **No maximum time limit** to complete a challenge—you may pass at your own pace.

### Why it matters

Drawdown rules protect firm capital and enforce discipline. One oversized loss can end funding even if your long-term idea was correct.

### How to stay safe

- Risk a **small, fixed percentage per trade** (many traders use 0.5%–1% of account equity).
- Use stop-losses and respect your **personal daily stop** well inside FundedFirm’s limit.
- Avoid revenge trading after a red day.
- Track **floating P&L**, not only closed trades.

Full detail: **Trading Rules → Trading Framework** (on the official rules page).

## 2. News trading (allowed at FundedFirm)

**Unlike many prop firms, FundedFirm allows news trading.** You may trade around central bank decisions, economic releases, and other high-impact events if that fits your strategy.

News can cause **slippage, widened spreads, and fast moves**. That is a market condition—not a ban on trading the news. You are expected to size positions responsibly and stay within drawdown limits.

### How to stay safe

- Use our **Forex Calculators** and economic calendar tools for your pairs.
- Reduce size or widen risk awareness before major releases if you are unsure.
- Never exceed daily or overall drawdown because of volatility.

Official policy: **Trade Without Constraints → News Trading Allowed**.

FundedFirm **does not restrict overnight or weekend holding**. Swing and multi-session strategies are allowed if they comply with drawdown and conduct rules.

## 4. Consistency and single-trade dependency

FundedFirm rewards **consistent, risk-managed trading**, not one lucky ticket.

Under our **Destructive Trading Habits** policy, accounts where **80% or more of total profit** comes from **a single trade** may be **reviewed**. The goal is sustainable performance, not one-off spikes.

Related conduct rules include:

- **No hedging the same instrument:** You cannot hold **both a buy and a sell** on the same pair at the same time (e.g. long and short XAUUSD together).
- **Lot-size consistency:** For each symbol, any new position must not exceed **5× the smallest lot size** you have used on that pair in your history (checked per instrument).

### How to stay safe

- Spread profit across multiple sessions when possible.
- Keep lot sizes stable relative to your plan.
- Follow a written trading plan instead of “all-in” entries.

Details: **Destructive Trading Habits** section on the rules page.

## 5. Risk management, position sizing, and forbidden practices

Beyond drawdown, FundedFirm monitors behavior that abuses the platform or breaks fair-trading standards.

### Position sizing and leverage

- Use leverage responsibly per our **Leverage Policy** and your account type.
- Respect **per-pair lot limits** (5× base lot rule above).
- Avoid concentrated exposure across **highly correlated** instruments.

### Strictly prohibited (examples)

FundedFirm may suspend or terminate accounts for practices including:

- **Copy trading / mirroring / coordinated trading** across accounts or users  
- **Hedging abuse between accounts**  
- **Bulk or clustered orders** that create artificial exposure  
- **Tick scalping / latency exploitation**  
- **Gambling-style** risk (oversized, reckless sizing, especially after losses)  
- **One-sided betting abuse** without adaptive risk management  

Expert Advisors (EAs) may be used where permitted by your program—see **FAQ** and your account terms. **Copy trading is not allowed.**

Enforcement and examples: **Forbidden Trading Practices** on the rules page.
